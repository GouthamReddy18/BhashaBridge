# Reviewed data

No human-reviewed annotation dataset is currently present. Do not place drafts here or interpret validator success as evidence of human review. Reviewed records require documented provenance and consistent review metadata under the guidelines.

Future benchmark candidates also require a reviewed English reference translation, reviewed `intended_meaning`, and explicit `split` plus group-stable `split_group` metadata. See [the evaluation protocol](../../docs/evaluation_protocol.md). Readiness metadata is not independent proof of reviewer identity, annotation quality, or licensing.
