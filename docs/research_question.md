The hypothesis is that selective preprocessing may help, but evaluation may also show no improvement or regressions. Compare direct translation, normalization plus translation, selective transliteration plus translation, and ambiguity-aware processing when compatible components exist. Report actual model versions, data, metrics, latency, and failures; do not infer causal gains from a few examples.
